Our Story

Craft. Fire.
Community.

Ember & Oak was born from a simple belief: that food cooked over fire, made with honest ingredients, and shared among people who care — is the most powerful thing a table can hold.

Founded by Chef Arjun Mehta and sommelier Priya Nair, our kitchen celebrates locally sourced produce, ancient wood-fire techniques, and a menu that changes with every season.
